"Christeffsky" to "Krestovskiy" and "Vassili Ostrov" to "Vasil’yevskiy ostrov": Merriman's placement and descriptions of these two St. Petersburg islands are accurate, but the names as printed match no spelling in readily-available contemporary references, such as period maps or the 1914 Baedeker for Russia. These have been corrected as printer's errors, not as a change in transliteration.

"Chorno-Ziom" to "Chernozem": No consulted reference gives this spelling. It appears to be a phonetic rendering of "chernozem" ("black earth") and has been corrected as a printer's error.

"Rtle" and "Dibutantes" were PG transcription errors in which accented characters were rendered as other, non-accented characters. These words were restored to "rôle" and "débutantes", then modernized to "role" and "debutantes" per Merriam-Webster.

Where the original, scanned text rendered a Russian word phonetically in an idiosyncratic way (e.g., "Nijni", "tchinovnik"), that spelling was changed to a standardized modern transliteration (using BGN/PCGN) and tagged as "[Editorial]". These are sound-alike substitutions of the same word. The three corrections under "Corrections of printer's errors" discussed above are different in kind; the printed forms are not variant transliterizations of the real names.
